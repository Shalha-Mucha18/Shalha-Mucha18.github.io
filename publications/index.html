Publications

BVQA: Connecting Language and Vision Through Multimodal Attention for Open-Ended Question Answering 2025

Venue: IEEE Xplore • Authors: Md. Shalha Mucha Bhuyan, Eftekhar Hossain, et al.

Visual Question Answering (VQA) requires joint understanding of natural language and visual content. We introduce a large-scale Bengali VQA (BVQA) dataset (~17.8k open-ended QA pairs across ~3.5k images), generated via in-context learning using LLMs from human-annotated captions. We also propose a Multimodal Cross-Attention Network (MCRAN) leveraging pretrained transformers, multi-head fusion, and a gating mechanism for robust vision-language alignment. Experiments on BVQA show improvements over baselines across multiple answer categories.

Advancing Bangla Sign Language Recognition Through Deep Learning Techniques 2025

Venue: NCIM 2025, Gazipur, Bangladesh — Accepted • Authors: Fahmida Ahamed Ifty, Md. Shalha Mucha Bhuyan, et al.

The importance of Bangla Sign Language (BdSL) is growing with an increasing user base; however, research remains limited. We propose an automated interpreter system and introduce a custom video dataset of 784 samples to support empirical study. A 3D CNN achieved 82.6% accuracy and robust performance across classes, validated with multiple metrics and tests on recorded videos.

AI-driven Phishing Website Detection using Ensemble Learning 2022

Supervisor: Prof. Jane Smith, Department of CSE, XYZ University

The thesis proposed a phishing detection system combining Random Forests, XGBoost, and Logistic Regression for robust URL classification. With 30+ extracted features, the ensemble achieved an F1-score of 94%. The system shows promise as a lightweight cybersecurity tool for early phishing detection.
