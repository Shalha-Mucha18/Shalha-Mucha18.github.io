2025 Publications
BVQA: Connecting Language and Vision Through Multimodal Attention for Open-Ended Question Answering 2025

Venue: IEEE Xplore • Authors: Md. Shalha Mucha Bhuyan, S. K. Sinha, N. Bansal, et al.

We present a multimodal attention framework aligning visual features with linguistic cues for open-ended VQA. The model leverages cross-modal fusion and hierarchical attention to improve generalization on diverse benchmarks.

Advancing Bangla Sign Language Recognition Through Deep Learning Techniques 2025

Venue: 2nd International Conference on Next-Generation Computing, IoT and Machine Learning (NCIM 2025), Gazipur-1707, Bangladesh — Accepted • Authors: Fahmida Ahamed Ifty, Md. Shalha Mucha Bhuyan, et al.

This work explores CNN- and transformer-based architectures for Bangla Sign Language recognition, focusing on data augmentation, domain adaptation, and robust evaluation for real-world deployment.
