Undergraduate Thesis • Md. Shalha Mucha Bhuyan • Supervisor: M. A. Rahaman • 2024
Preprocessing (denoise, normalize) → fuzzy C-means segmentation → handcrafted features → neuro-fuzzy classifier. Achieved measurable gains over KNN baselines on a curated MRI set.
